Where the numbers come from

  • Earn rates are the app's card terms (core/earn_rules_seed.py). Cards marked terms not confirmed were copied from the old catalog and haven't been checked against current terms yet.
  • Travel values: Frequent Miler's Reasonable Redemption Values, read 5 November 2026. These are estimates of typical award value, not amounts you've redeemed.
  • Cash-out rates: Chase 1¢; Amex 0.6¢ as a statement credit; Citi 0.75¢ from a Strata Premier account since August 2025 and 1¢ from Custom Cash or Double Cash. Citi has told cardholders that Strata Premier points in the same ThankYou account as a Custom Cash or Double Cash also cash out at 1¢ (Doctor of Credit, 2025, not reconfirmed); this page uses 0.75¢, which you can change in the values table. Capital One 0.5¢; Bilt 0.55¢ (Upgraded Points).
  • Minimum values are the least a travel booking always gets: Chase 1¢ (cash or Chase Travel), Amex 1¢ (flights paid with points on Amex Travel), Citi 1¢ (Citi Travel), Capital One 1¢ (erasing a travel purchase), Bilt 1.25¢ (Bilt Travel). Hotel and airline points have no floor, so they keep Frequent Miler's value.
  • For travel, Freedom points count as transferable because they can move to a Sapphire Preferred, and Custom Cash and Double Cash points because they can move to the Strata Premier.
  • Foreign transaction fees (3% on the Freedom cards, Custom Cash, Double Cash, BofA and U.S. Bank; 2.7% on Blue Business Plus) are taken off the restaurants-abroad row. The app doesn't store these fees yet.
  • Not counted, because the app has no terms set up for them: Fidelity Rewards, Amex Cash Magnet, Atmos Summit, Best Buy, Rakuten and the store cards.
  • This compares the cards' published terms. It isn't advice from a licensed financial advisor.
